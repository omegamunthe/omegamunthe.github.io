Portfolio Description

Description: Developed a real-time activity and performance monitoring system to improve marketing productivity, operational visibility, and data-driven decision-making. TITEN integrates activity logbooks and operational data from Google Forms and Google Sheets into a centralized dashboard, enabling end-to-end monitoring of marketing activities, order-in (OI) generation, application processing, and productivity across business portfolios.

Key Contributions:

  • Developed a real-time activity and performance monitoring system to improve marketing productivity, operational visibility, and data-driven decision-making. TITEN integrates activity logbooks and operational data from Google Forms and Google Sheets into a centralized dashboard, enabling end-to-end monitoring of marketing activities, order-in (OI) generation, application processing, and productivity across business portfolios
  • Designed an interactive dashboard to monitor activity volume, daily productivity, activity trends, and performance across teams, areas, and business portfolios.
  • Developed an end-to-end monitoring approach connecting marketing activities with order generation and subsequent application processing stages.
  • Enabled performance tracking across key operational indicators, including activity completion, OI-to-PPD progress, application cancellations, and conversion performance.
  • Supported cross-functional coordination between Credit and Marketing to identify process bottlenecks and address human-error-related cancellations through standardized application input guidance and data refreshment.
  • Established a more structured basis for evaluating individual and team productivity against operational targets, including a minimum target of four activities per day for the relevant Channeling workflow.

Tools & Methodology:

  • Data Source: Google Forms, Google Sheets, activity logbooks, Marketing Order Register (MOR), and cancellation records
  • Tools: Google Workspace, dashboarding and spreadsheet-based data processing
  • Methodology: Activity tracking, KPI monitoring, operational funnel analysis, productivity measurement, and cross-functional performance evaluation

Business Impact:

  • Performance Visibility: Improved visibility into marketing activities and productivity through centralized monitoring.
  • Data-Driven Evaluation: Enabled stakeholders to compare activity trends and performance across periods, teams, and business portfolios.
  • Process Improvement: Supported the identification of application processing bottlenecks and human-error-related cancellation issues.
  • Productivity Management: Provided a structured basis for monitoring individual activity performance against daily targets.
  • Operational Alignment: Strengthened coordination between Marketing and Credit by connecting activity monitoring with application pipeline performance.
