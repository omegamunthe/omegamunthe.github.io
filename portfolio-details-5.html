  • Project URL: SAE, Smart Alternative Earnings - A Proxy Income Approach

Portfolio Description

Description: Developed a data-driven, rule-based decision-support tool to estimate customers' income capacity using historical credit data from SLIK (Indonesia's credit information system). The tool addresses challenges in assessing applicants who cannot provide formal proof of income by leveraging repayment patterns and existing credit obligations to generate a proxy income estimate. It aims to support more consistent credit assessments while maintaining risk controls and credit policy compliance.

Key Contributions:

  • Analyzed historical credit data to develop a structured approach for estimating income capacity based on customers' existing installment obligations and repayment patterns.
  • Designed a proxy income calculation framework using the largest qualifying installment amount, weighted by SLIK classification, payment stability, and repayment capacity
  • Incorporated risk control mechanisms involving credit bureau integrity checks, overdue status, SLIK history depth, contract duration, and active installment obligations
  • Developed an automated decision-support tool to process structured Excel data and calculate proxy income estimates based on predefined rules
  • Translated credit assessment requirements into standardized business rules to improve consistency and reduce reliance on subjective income estimation
  • Designed the solution to support credit analysts in evaluating eligible applications without replacing the final credit approval decision

Tools & Methodology:

  • Data Source: SLIK / credit history data and structured Excel files
  • Tools: Python-based web application (Streamlit), Excel data processing
  • Methodology: Rule-based proxy income estimation, installment pattern analysis, repayment capacity assessment, and risk-based adjustment

Expect Business Impact:

  • Process Efficiency: Simplifies income assessment for eligible applicants without formal income documentation
  • Assessment Consistency: Establishes a standardized, rule-based approach to estimating repayment capacity
  • Risk Control: Incorporates predefined risk filters to flag or exclude cases requiring additional verification
  • Decision Support: Helps credit analysts make more consistent, data-informed assessments while maintaining credit risk safeguards
